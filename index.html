How to play

  1. Tap an empty square to plant an apple.
  2. The snake moves on its own, one square at a time, toward that apple. It grows when it eats.
  3. Tap ahead of time to queue the next apple. Tap that square again to clear it. With nothing queued, the snake waits and the clock stops.
  4. Kill it on a wall or its own body before every pip fills. A full set means it escapes.

Keys: 1–4 snakes, R restart, M mute, − and = speed.

Ekans is snake, spelled backward. The serpent moves. You only choose the bait.
